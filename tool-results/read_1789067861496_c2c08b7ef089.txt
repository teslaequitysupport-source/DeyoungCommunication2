   383→   383→7. WORKER FAILURE RECOVERY
   384→   384→
   385→   385→If a worker stops responding:
   386→   386→
   387→   387→1. Detect heartbeat failure.
   388→   388→2. Mark worker unhealthy.
   389→   389→3. Stop assigning new work.
   390→   390→4. Determine whether the current job can be recovered.
   391→   391→5. Retry if safe.
   392→   392→6. Reassign when possible.
   393→   393→7. Record the incident.
   394→   394→8. Notify the session manager.
   395→   395→9. Restore the session where technically possible.
   396→   396→10. Never pretend the worker is still running.
   397→   397→
   398→   398→Use idempotency so retries do not duplicate jobs.
