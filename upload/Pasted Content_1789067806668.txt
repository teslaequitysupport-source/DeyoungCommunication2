7. WORKER FAILURE RECOVERY

If a worker stops responding:

1. Detect heartbeat failure.
2. Mark worker unhealthy.
3. Stop assigning new work.
4. Determine whether the current job can be recovered.
5. Retry if safe.
6. Reassign when possible.
7. Record the incident.
8. Notify the session manager.
9. Restore the session where technically possible.
10. Never pretend the worker is still running.

Use idempotency so retries do not duplicate jobs.
